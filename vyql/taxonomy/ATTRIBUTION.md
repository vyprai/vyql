# Attribution — CWE and CAPEC

`cwe.json` and `capec.json` are derived from the MITRE Common Weakness
Enumeration (CWE) and Common Attack Pattern Enumeration and Classification
(CAPEC) catalogs. They include CWE and CAPEC identifiers, names, descriptions
and relationships as published by MITRE, reshaped into the JSON form this engine
loads at runtime.
